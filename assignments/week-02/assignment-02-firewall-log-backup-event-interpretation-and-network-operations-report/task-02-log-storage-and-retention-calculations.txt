Given daily uncompressed volumes:
- Edge firewall: 2.8 GB/day
- Internal firewall: 1.6 GB/day
- VPN gateway: 0.7 GB/day
- DMZ firewall: 0.9 GB/day

A. TOTAL DAILY LOG VOLUME

2.8 + 1.6 + 0.7 + 0.9 = 6.0 GB/day

Answer: 6.0 GB/day uncompressed.

B. COMPRESSED DAILY STORAGE REQUIREMENT

A 60% reduction means that 40% of the original volume remains.

6.0 × 0.40 = 2.4 GB/day

Answer: 2.4 GB/day compressed.

C. COMPRESSED STORAGE FOR 90 DAYS

2.4 × 90 = 216 GB

Answer: 216 GB.

D. COMPRESSED STORAGE FOR 365 DAYS

2.4 × 365 = 876 GB

Answer: 876 GB.

E. MONTHLY STORAGE COST FOR THE 365-DAY ARCHIVE VOLUME

876 × ₦18 = ₦15,768 per month

Answer: ₦15,768 per month.

F. DAILY UNCOMPRESSED VOLUME AFTER TWO QUARTERS OF 12% GROWTH

Assumption: growth compounds at the end of each quarter.

6.0 × 1.12 × 1.12 = 7.5264 GB/day

Answer: approximately 7.53 GB/day uncompressed.

G. RESTORE TIME FOR 1 TB

Assumption: 1 TB = 1,000 GB for this storage calculation.

1,000 ÷ 100 = 10 groups of 100 GB

10 × 35 minutes = 350 minutes

350 minutes = 5 hours 50 minutes

Answer: approximately 5 hours 50 minutes.

Alternative binary-storage assumption:
If 1 TB is treated as 1,024 GB, the result is 358.4 minutes, or approximately 5 hours 58 minutes.
